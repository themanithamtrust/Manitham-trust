MANITHAM TRUST
Save Humanity, create an immaculate society

Manitham is a social work organization based at Trichy. Mr.R.Dinesh Kumar B.C.A., M.S.W., PGDC is the founder & Managing Trustee for this organization. The moto of this organization is to develop our community with minimum political knowledge. We have started our manitham social work organization on 1st June 2012 and it was registered by 3rd 2018. We provide motivational training, skill development training, career guidance for students, and evening study centers for poor children.

The reason behind the name "Manitham" (Humanity) stems from the changing values in today’s society, where the tradition of living together as families and the spirit of helping others is gradually diminishing. A human being is born not just for themselves, but to live for others. However, this sense of shared humanity is fading...

Our Vision

Save Humanity, to create an immaculate society

Our Mission

A) To promote aged care and help homeless & abandoned aged people.

B) To provide Education, Health and Food for Orphan children.

C) Support Women Empowerment and Youth Career Growth.

D) Support socially and economically backward community.

E) Promote political education among youth and teenage people.

F) Preserve Environment and Natural Resources.

Objectives

To establish programmes for poor people including self-employment, education, food, clothing etc.
To help poor people to become self-reliant.
To start activities to improve socio-economic standards for women, children, orphans and marginalized groups.
To help women and children to be self-reliant.
To prevent exploitation of children and enforce their rights including abolition of child labour.
